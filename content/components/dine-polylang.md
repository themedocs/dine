After installing Polylang, you'll see Header and Footer are missing. Here're steps to recover them.

1. Visit **Dashboard > Languages > Settings > Custom post types and Taxonomies** and check for DINE Blocks. ![](/dine/assets/dine_polylang1.jpg)
2. Go to **Dashboard > DINE Blocks** > edit your Header block. Do nothing, just hit **Update**. ![](/dine/assets/dine_polylang2.jpg)
3. Apply the same for other blocks: Footer, Transparent Header, Off-canvas.. all in your DINE Blocks list.
